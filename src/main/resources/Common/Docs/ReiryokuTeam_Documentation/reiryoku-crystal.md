# Reiryoku Crystal

Reiryoku Crystals are the essential material for progression in this mod.

To craft a Reiryoku Crystal, you need two things. **Hollow Essence** and the **Arcanist's Bench**. After that in the
Arcanist's Bench>Misc you will find the crafting recipe for the Reiryoku Crystal.

![Reiryoku Crystal Crafting](UI/Custom/Docs/Images/reiryoku-crystal_crafting.png)

The Reiryoku Crystal is used for almost everything in this mod. It is an essential part of the gameplay loop. And for
now it replaces what would later be locked behind levels and skill-points instead of crafts.

With the Reiryoku Crystal you will finally be able to progress further than sealed form.

## Hollow Essence: How to obtain

The **Hollow Essence** is obtained by defeating the various Hollow-Type enemies that this mod has to offer.
Currently, Hollows spawn at Night-time outside of Zone 1. All hollow-type enemies have a **50% chance** of dropping 1
hollow essence.
