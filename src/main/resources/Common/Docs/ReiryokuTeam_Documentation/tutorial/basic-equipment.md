# Basic Equipment

In this guide you will learn how to craft the basic equipment, as well as how to use some of it.

This includes Sealed Zanpakuto, Kojaku Bow (Basic Quincy Bow) and the 4-point Quincy Cross.

## Sealed Zanpakuto

In order to craft Sealed Zanpakuto you need the **Blacksmith Anvil**, there craft first an **Asauchi** which
is the vanilla bench for crafting weapons.

There at tier one you can see all the Sealed Zanpakuto. This list for now includes:
- Sealed - Zangetsu
- Sealed -  Sode no Shirayuki
- Sealed - Benihime
- Sealed - Wabisuke
- Sealed - Hozukimaru
- Sealed - Senbonzakura
- Sealed - Kenpachi Sword (Sealed Nozarashi), tier 2 bench required.

![Sealed-Crafting](UI/Custom/Docs/Images/sealed_crafting.png)

_Note: Life Essence is obtained by gathering crops_

Right now, most of the Sealed Zanpakuto don't have any special characteristics besides having **infinite durability**.
But Kenjutsu-Styles are planned for the future. _(An exception to this is Sealed Nozarashi, as it is as powerful as a
shikai weapon, therefore harder to craft)_

All the Sealed Zanpakuto are all capable of transforming into **_Shikai_** after consuming their
respective _Spirit Fragment_.

For all of the following progress regarding the unlocking of **Shikai** and even crafting the various items
this mod has to offer, check out the **Reiryoku Crystal** entry.

## Quincy Equipment

As mentioned above, there is two piece of Quincy Equipment currently available. 
With more to come in the future of course.

We have:
- Quincy Bow - Kojaku
- 4-point Quincy Cross

The 4-point Quincy Cross is crafted at the Workbench>Tools section while Kojaku is crafted at the
Blacksmith's Anvil>Bow section.

![Kojaku Crafting Recipe and Cross Crafting Recipe](UI/Custom/Docs/Images/quincybow_crafting.png)

### How to use the Quincy Bow (Kojaku)


**Kojaku** is a bow that instead of arrows utilizes Mana. That is where the **4-Point Quincy Cross** comes into place.

The **4-Point Quincy Cross** is an OFF-HAND item. Meaning you need to equip it into your off-hand. After equipping the
cross you will recieve a bonus of +50MP, allowing you to use the Kojaku Bow. --- 

_Warning: Having the cross in your off-hand while holding a melee weapon will prevent you from blocking and performing
certain actions. It is recommended to use it only with the Kojaku Bow._

---------------
#### Kojaku Bow Controls


**Shoot (Left-Click)**: Hold left click to charge your arrows, as any other bow. This will consume MP depending on how much
you charge your arrow, and the more you charge your arrow the more damage you deal. 
There are 5 charging stages, and they consume the following [4, 8, 12, 16, 19]

**Hirenkyaku (Right-Click)**: Press to perform Hirenkyaku (_dash_), this consumes 5 MP

**Volley (E)**: This is a secondary skill. Requires 50% Signature Charge. After pressing E you need to charge an attack
on your bow, and you will shoot 3 arrows at the same time.

**Ōkami Ya (大神矢) (Q)**: This is the signature move. Requires 100% Signature Charge. After activating this state your 
bow will look significantly larger as Reishi concentrates on it. Charge your bow to launch a devastating arow at great
speed.
